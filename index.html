My Favourite Academic Essays

Defending Canada Culture Against American Mass Media

Canadian Fiction, Winter 2026

This essay examines Elizabeth Smart's high modernist novel, By Grand Central Station I Sat Down and Wept, as a strategic defence against the mid-20th-century American invasion of Canadian Culture. By analyzing the text alongside the 1951 Massey Report, the paper argues that Smart's literary innovation serves as a direct rebuttal to the cultural anxieties regarding "permanent dependence" on American mass media.

The politics of place and time: Religious-Induced Hysteria in 17th Century New England

American Literature, Winter 2026

This essay investigates the intersection of landscape, politics, and religious hysteria in 17th-century New England. By examining the defining influence of Puritanism, the paper analyzes how the physical environment was utilized as a tool to cultivate and critique religious paranoia.
